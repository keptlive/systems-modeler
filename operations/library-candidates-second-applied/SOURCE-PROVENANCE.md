# Source and adaptation boundaries

Prepared 2026-10-24. These ten JSON files are candidate mathematical teaching reconstructions, not active-library admissions. Parameters, initial values, equations, horizon and solver are enumerated in MANIFEST.json. Original descriptive prose distinguishes source laws from authored scenarios.

## Retained openly licensed source

* python-control revision `b4cf7360138985f84afe299337ef9ee6c25e9c38`, `examples/pvtol.py`: BSD 3-Clause, copyright California Institute of Technology, Delft University of Technology and python-control developers. Complete source and license retained in sources/. Plant parameters and force clipping are preserved. The small-angle feedback gains and initial disturbance are our teaching choices. The model does not reproduce a source optimal-control trajectory.
* ScalableTestSuite revision `2e288e71eb0a7834310827144462726180d10500`, `ScalableTestSuite/Thermal/HeatExchanger.mo`: BSD 3-Clause, copyright2015 Politecnico di Milano. Complete source and license retained in sources/. Our nine-state specialization uses N4, constant inlet/flow conditions, chosen thermal capacities and conductances. Source midpoint film temperatures and wall-storage equations are retained. Source cpW is labelled specific heat while used as total thermal capacitance; we explicitly define effective per-section WallCapacity in J/K rather than claiming that label is dimensionally correct. No spatial convergence or hardware calibration claim.

## Referenced mathematics; source text/code not redistributed

* [University-hosted CTM motor tutorial](https://www.ece.ualberta.ca/~tchen/ctm/examples/motor/motor.html), dated1997 on page: source state matrix and six numerical physical constants, zero-state1V experiment. Page accessible through web reader. Source J unit text appears erroneous; canonical inertia interpretation is kg*m^2. We do not reproduce the MATLAB or page prose. No explicit open redistribution license established for that page.
* [University-hosted CTM suspension tutorial](https://www.ece.ualberta.ca/~tchen/ctm/examples/susp/susp.html): source Newton-law two-mass plant and six numerical constants. Web search returned primary page full text; direct opening intermittently timed out. The smooth road protocol is authored, not a claimed source trace. Tire damping includes road velocity, avoiding the missing impulse of a naive road-height step. No explicit open redistribution license established; no source text/code retained.
* [APMonitor stirred reactor](https://apmonitor.com/pdc/index.php/Main/StirredReactor): full primary page accessible. Source mass/energy laws, constants and coolant300K equilibrium. Our time origin moves to the295K cooling step. Source comments mix seconds/minutes and physical scales; our coherent numerical convention is L, g, mol, J, K, min. No claim to reproduce the original pre-step timeline or a reactor controller. No explicit open redistribution license established for the page; no page prose/code retained.
* [APMonitor distillation](https://apmonitor.com/wiki/index.php/Apps/DistillationColumn): full primary page accessible, citing Hahn and Edgar2002, Computers and Chemical Engineering26,1379–1397. We use only its GEKKO branch: feed2, feed fraction0.5, volatility1.6, condenser0.5, tray0.25, reboiler0.1, steady ratio0.7 then ratio3. The page also contains a different legacy APM branch; its initials/holdups are not mixed in. The initial steady profile is independently solved, not copied from an inconsistent initial table. No explicit open redistribution license established; no source prose/code retained.
* [CTMS boost activity](https://ctms.engin.umich.edu/CTMS/index.php?aux=Activities_BoostcircuitA): primary search result provides ON/OFF Kirchhoff balances and capacitor explanation. Direct URL variants may have access restrictions. The period-averaged ideal continuous-conduction model is an explicitly authored closure from those laws; chosen components and duty0.5→0.6 experiment are authored. ESR, losses, discrete switch ripple and discontinuous conduction are omitted. This is not a source published averaged benchmark. No explicit open page license established; no source text/code retained.
* [Harvard Hank Yang, Optimal Control and Estimation, equations6.31–6.32](https://hankyang.seas.harvard.edu/OptimalControlEstimation/output-feedback.html): full primary page accessible. General continuous observer/Riccati laws specialized to the authored constant-velocity/multisine demonstration. No stochastic noise sampler is used; deterministic error is not interpreted as a stochastic realization, and design P is not a calibrated confidence interval. No explicit open page license established in this review; no text/code retained.
