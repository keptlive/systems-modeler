# Independent peer review — environmental candidates

Decision: **approve these ten exact candidate hashes for admission as explicitly illustrative process-law adaptations**, subject to the integrating maintainer's normal catalog review. No mandatory equation, unit, numerical, duplicate or rights correction was found. This is not approval to describe them as ten imported published field models, calibrated forecasts, complete PDE implementations or the original authors' numerical experiments. No candidate or active model was edited in this review.

## Duplicate utility and limits

The closest existing groundwater models use scalar recharge/extraction/storage rules; they do not have signed squared-head exchange between two hydraulic cells. Existing Greenland/ice-sheet entries use temperature-driven mass balance rather than fixed-slope fifth-power discharge. Existing ocean deoxygenation is a temperature/stratification proxy rather than the BOD/reaeration sag mechanism. The sensor/control and biology additions now in the948-entry inventory do not duplicate these laws.

Conservative fingerprints are an aid, not a proof of general algebraic inequivalence. The relevant distinction here is both the implemented mechanism and its teaching evidence. Source law attribution does not make the deliberately added closures source-authored. The batch makes this distinction adequately. Cases requiring spatial gradients, precision dating, complete particle dissolution, rainfall limitation, oxygen-limited chemistry or field calibration must remain outside the stated capability.

Rights review found original mathematical expressions and original explanatory prose, with no article text, figures, third-party code or datasets packaged for redistribution. Publisher access and public document links do not establish a blanket reuse license. No such blanket license is claimed. The source-access failures above are recorded as limitations rather than replaced with claims to have read unavailable full texts.
