# Healthcare source reconciliation 1.4

Nine failed healthcare defaults were reviewed. Eight remain mathematically unresolved; their equations and numeric inputs were preserved. Citation repair does not certify scientific trust. The pharmacokinetic model received a bounded mechanistic correction with independent numerical verification; its parameters remain illustrative, not drug-specific.

## Pharmacokinetic repair

The original references Drug_GI, Drug_Central and Drug_Peripheral did not name any declared stock. Their replacements point to the original existing stock labels, preserving IDs and positions. Original rates, 100mg oral amount, bioavailability0.85 and volume10L remain unchanged. Units are now explicit (h, mg, L, mg/L).

Torsten documents G'= -Ka G, C'=Ka G -(CL/Vcentral+Q/Vcentral)C +(Q/Vperipheral)P, P'=(Q/Vcentral)C -(Q/Vperipheral)P. Here Kel=CL/Vcentral, K12=Q/Vcentral, K21=Q/Vperipheral. We retain an explicit full oral GI amount and split its first-order departure into systemic absorption F Ka G and non-systemic loss (1-F)Ka G. This is a mass-conserving realization of bioavailability as extent, rather than multiplying the only GI departure by F (which eventually makes100% systemically available). The added loss flow preserves the existing absorption path and all existing flows.

The slower disposition eigenvalue beta satisfies beta^2 -(Kel+K12+K21)beta+Kel*K21=0. Half Life now uses ln(2)/beta. It measures disposition; a sufficiently slow Ka can dominate the observed oral terminal slope. The existing thresholds2 and10mg/L remain arbitrary educational values; they have no identified drug or therapeutic evidence.

Nine tests passed. Closed-form convolution independently checks the full24h trajectory for F=0,0.85,1, positivity, concentration, eigenmode half-life and an analytic accounting identity. A separate clone-only test adds cumulative sink stocks and connects the actual unchanged elimination and non-systemic loss flows to them. For F=0,0.85,1, it checks every integrated cumulative sink against its closed form and total mass across all five actual stocks against100mg at every timestep (absolute tolerance2e-11mg). No on-disk model topology is altered by this test. The default Euler solution is within2mg of the reference and improves by more than40% with a halved step. Integrated plasma AUC through256h matches F*dose/(Kel*Volume)=42.5mg*h/L within0.02%. RK4 uses1/64h to satisfy2e-6mg absolute amount tolerance. This verifies the mathematical realization, not clinical validity.

## Remaining findings

- models/healthcare/antibiotic-resistance-evolution-extended-hea.json: The corrected Laxminarayan et al. citation is a commission review, not the source of these ODEs. All bacterial stocks and effectiveness start at zero, giving 0/0 in Effectiveness_Decay. Drug_Approval transfers an amount of pipeline drugs directly to dimensionless effectiveness; no unit conversion or source for coefficients/initial values is established. No guessed bacterial seed or effectiveness was inserted.
- models/healthcare/body-temperature-thermoregulation-hea.json: The unverified journal DOI has been replaced by the publisher record for the named general physiology textbook. No textbook equation, heat-unit convention or parameter mapping was established. Heat_Content=1000 and Thermal_Capacitance=27 imply 37.037 degrees, but heat production and loss units/time conversion are unspecified. The model name Core_Response_Time appears to be a copied variable name; its original label is preserved. No physically unsupported conversion was guessed.
- models/healthcare/burnout-recovery-cycle-hea.json: Maslach et al. defines exhaustion, cynicism and inefficacy; the energy/stress/burnout stock system is an illustrative construction, not a reproduced or calibrated burnout measure. At the initial state energy restoration is 2 and drain is 42 per model time; energy derivative is -40. No source justifies the scales, time unit, boundary behavior or coefficients, and adding arbitrary clipping would not validate them.
- models/healthcare/chronic-disease-progression-model-hea.json: The previous DOI is Muntaner's political-health commentary, not a chronic-disease stock-flow source. No identified disease, cohort, transition matrix, screening-effect calibration or QALY time basis supports these coefficients. Healthy/At_Risk/Undiagnosed aliases do not match stock labels; changing aliases alone would not establish disease-specific validity.
- models/healthcare/healthcare-capacity-surge-hea.json: The previous DOI is Muntaner's political-health commentary, not a capacity model source. Burnout_Rate is undefined although Base_Burnout_Rate exists. Admissions compares demand per time against an available-bed count without a stated admission time, and Effective_Capacity can become negative. No source or unit convention establishes the intended replacement, so coefficients and flows are preserved.
- models/healthcare/hospital-capacity-and-patient-flow-model-hea.json: The previous DOI is Muntaner's political-health commentary, not a hospital patient-flow source. Several stock/parameter aliases do not match declared labels; ICU allocation and general admissions require topology and capacity reconciliation. No hospital data, length-of-stay distribution, mortality calibration or fatigue/quality causal law is traced to a source. No arbitrary alias-only repair is presented as scientific validation.
- models/healthcare/mental-health-treatment-system-dynamics-hea.json: The previous DOI is Muntaner's political-health commentary, not a mental-health treatment model source. Disorder groups, treatment/dropout aliases, workforce indices and stigma scales have no source mapping. The displayed DALY expression weights current populations without explicit time integration; it does not by itself establish disability-adjusted life-years. No empirical incidence, treatment success or mortality calibration is established.
- models/healthcare/two-compartment-pharmacokinetics-model-hea.json: The invalid book DOI was replaced by the publisher record of Gibaldi/Perrier (1982), a conceptual reference. The mechanistic equations were reconciled to the published Torsten two-compartment mass ODEs: Kel=CL/Vcentral, K12=Q/Vcentral, K21=Q/Vperipheral. Undeclared shorthand references now use existing stock names. An explicit non-systemic GI loss of Ka*GI*(1-F) makes GI depletion Ka*GI and systemic input F*Ka*GI, so F controls extent rather than only speed. Half Life now gives the slower disposition eigenmode, not ln(2)/Kel; if absorption is slower it need not equal the observed oral terminal half-life. Stocks are amounts (mg), volume L, time h, rates 1/h and concentration mg/L. Existing numerical constants and the 100 mg oral amount are illustrative, with no identified drug, fit or clinical therapeutic range; the existing therapeutic status diagnostic is not clinical advice.
- models/healthcare/vaccine-allocation-optimization-hea.json: The former DOI is Castillo et al.'s vaccine-market-design article; Bubar et al.'s correct DOI is now provided for conceptual context. This definition has fixed three-group allocation fractions and no epidemic/contact/serostatus dynamics, policy search or optimization objective; it is not Bubar's age-structured transmission model. Lives Saved multiplies doses by efficacy and IFR without infection-risk exposure or a counterfactual epidemic, so it is not an established estimate of deaths averted. All vaccination totals begin at zero, yielding 0/0 in Deaths per Million Vaccines. No ad hoc denominator guard or assumed infection risk was added.
