# Library curation

The curated public library contains **923 examples: 920 dynamic models and three static calculators**. This is a quality target, not a padded count of 1,000. Historical audit counts refer to the pre-curation library.

## Review and decisions

All 1,078 prior definitions were rerun at their complete default horizons with seed 42. All 28 recorded failures reproduced. The review also found 99 flowless definitions and 28 models whose every flow was literal zero. Numerical completion had hidden those missing dynamics. Flat trajectories alone were not grounds for removal: valid equilibrium/baseline examples, including Atmospheric Bathtub, remain.

Five flowless examples were [reconstructed and checked against independent mathematics](MODEL-RECONSTRUCTIONS-2026-10.md). Three useful fixed-input calculators remain, explicitly labeled as static: Kingman queue approximation, photovoltaic temperature correction, and traffic fundamental diagram. The other 91 flowless stubs were retired. Three rounded, near-identical Lorenz variants were retired in favor of the more precise example and source-tested convection model. Five broken defaults were also retired: unused initial populations, missing position integration, absent worker populations, and a pulse outside its saved horizon. Some are repairable; retirement does not claim the underlying scientific idea is invalid.

The [manifest](../operations/library-curation.json) records every individual decision, original path, SHA-256 and archived location. Exact original definitions remain under tests/fixtures/retired-library, outside every runtime model-discovery path. Historical source and counterexample tests still read them explicitly. They are evidence, not active catalog entries. No user-created production definitions are included in this retirement list.

## Engine lessons

The retired failures retain tests for ambiguous symbols, undefined forcing/functions, zero denominators, invalid domains and overflow, including causal root/dependency reporting. We did not hide invalid equations with clamps or silently reinterpret flows. Existing disconnected-stock warnings are now part of library admission checks. A new warning identifies models whose stock-changing flows are all literal zero; intentional fixed baselines remain legal. Audits reject empty inventories/output and now record input issues and changing stocks separately from numerical completion.

The [Simlin comparison](../operations/simlin-review.md) identified an actual XMILE interoperability defect: an empty dimensions container was incorrectly rejected as an array. That narrow case now imports, while real arrays remain explicitly unsupported. Attributed MIT SDXorg SIR/Teacup fixtures match their complete reference series. They were not added to the catalog because they duplicate existing examples. See [open-model provenance](OPEN-MODEL-PROVENANCE.md).

## Validation boundary

Every active definition must complete its default horizon with finite output. No active model has an expected-failure exemption. Reconstruction tests additionally check analytic solutions, conservation, energy and independent ODE references. Three static calculators have formula and changed-input tests. These checks do not establish calibration or scientific validity of every remaining model; preserved illustrative models must be judged against their assumptions and sources.
