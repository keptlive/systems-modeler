# Physical and economic source reconciliation 1.4

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Eighteen model files receive bibliographic corrections and/or precise limitations in their descriptions. No equations, stock initial values, variables/inputs, IDs, display names, connectors, layout or time settings changed. `operations/physical-source-reconciliation-v14.json` records exact before/after SHA-256 hashes, every changed field, previous and replacement content, primary evidence URLs and limitations.

## Bibliographic recovery and scientific limits

- Lighthill-Whitham traffic Part II is DOI **10.1098/rspa.1955.0089**, not Part I flood movement ending 0088. Both traffic files now cite Part II, while identifying their lumped-stock/queue equations as illustrative rather than a verified LWR spatial discretization. [Original publisher](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rspa.1955.0089).
- Greenshields (1935) is the [original TRB paper](https://onlinepubs.trb.org/Onlinepubs/hrbproceedings/14/14P1-023.pdf), identified by [TRB catalogue 120649](https://trid.trb.org/view/120649). DOI 10.17226/22229 did not identify it. The catalogue gives volume 14 Part I, pp.448-477 including discussion. Differentiating the file's closure q=vf*rho*(1-rho/rhoj) gives capacity vf*rhoj/4 at rhoj/2. That validates only the closure algebra, not the queue, forcing, time step or calibration.
- Tyson-Novak (2001), full cell-cycle title and pages, is DOI **10.1006/jtbi.2001.2293** ([record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11371178/)); the former DOI identified Cats. The local four-stock file has no established mapping to a particular published equation set. Distinct k3/K3, k4/K4, k5/K5 and k6/K6 symbols must not be merged.
- [Rothermel (1972)](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/32533) is USDA INT-115, DOI **10.2737/INT-RP-115**. The original uses a heat-source/heat-sink spread formulation; the file's wind/fuel/humidity multipliers are heuristic. Correcting its citation does not make those equations Rothermel's model.
- [Rayleigh (1916)](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/14786441608635602) is DOI **10.1080/14786441608635602**. The file's amplitude-growth stock is not the original boundary-value stability calculation. Its fixed threshold 1708 needs specified boundary conditions; amplitude lacks nonlinear saturation and thermal feedback.
- [Smets-Wouters (2007)](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.97.3.586) is DOI **10.1257/aer.97.3.586**. The local three-variable, fixed-expectations adjustment equations are not their estimated stochastic rational-expectations DSGE system.
- [Rysman (2009)](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.23.3.125) is DOI **10.1257/jep.23.3.125**, a conceptual overview of two-sided markets. The local platform acquisition, churn and perceived-value assumptions have no demonstrated equation mapping to it.
- Courchamp et al. (1999) is DOI **10.1016/S0169-5347(99)01683-3** ([original text](https://webpages.ciencias.ulisboa.pt/~mcgomes/aulas/dinpop/Temas/Courcham%20et%20al%201999%20Inverse%20density%20dependence%20and%20the%20Allee%20effect.pdf)). The file's N/(A+N) factor is weak Allee dynamics: without predators per-capita growth is positive for every 0<N<K. Its previous minimum-viable-population-threshold claim was unsupported. Lowercase a is attack rate; uppercase A is the Allee scale.
- Vollenweider (1968) is identified by the [EPA report record](https://heronetnext.epa.gov/reference/37262/) and [author retrospective](https://garfield.library.upenn.edu/classics1987/A1987J648000001.pdf), not the stored pied-flycatcher DOI. Full original report equations were not inspected. Algal phosphorus uptake and stoichiometry are absent from this local three-stock file.
- Sarmiento-Gruber (2006) is [Ocean Biogeochemical Dynamics](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt3fgxqx), DOI **10.2307/j.ctt3fgxqx**; the publisher supplies [chapter 10](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/chapters/s10_8223.pdf). Both carbon files now identify the named book. Equation/table mapping and parameter provenance remain unverified.
- Zeebe-Wolf-Gladrow (2001) is confirmed by the [author bibliography](https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/oceanography/faculty/zeebe_files/Publications.html). The acidification file now links that bibliographic evidence instead of an unrelated clay-weathering DOI. Carbonate-equilibrium, alkalinity, pH-scale and parameter fidelity remain unresolved.
- Field-Noyes (1974), *Oscillations in chemical systems. IV. Limit cycle behavior in a model of a real chemical reaction*, JCP60(5):1877-1884, is DOI **10.1063/1.1681288** ([original paper](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jcp/article-pdf/60/5/1877/18889964/1877_1_online.pdf)). The physics BZ variant already had the correct DOI, but now discloses unmapped effective rates. Its initial dX/dt=-2001986 and dt=.01 cause an Euler step to overshoot below zero; no coefficient rescaling was invented. The other/Oregonator file had a coil-stretch DOI and no flows or stock derivatives. It is now described as an incomplete scaffold. No replacement oscillator was silently inserted.

## Checks and remaining work

Three additional economics corrections recover [Mortensen-Pissarides (1994)](https://cep.lse.ac.uk/_NEW/PUBLICATIONS/abstract.asp?index=6904), DOI **10.2307/2297896**, [Feld (2012)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/9781119204459), DOI **10.1002/9781119204459**, and the original [ECB semiconductor-shortage analysis](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/economic-bulletin/focus/2021/html/ecb.ebbox202104_06~780de2a8fb.en.html), correcting its full title to *The semiconductor shortage and its implication for euro area trade, production and prices*, Issue 4/2021, Box 6. Their previous DOIs identified job-security provisions, supply-chain knowledge spillovers, and perishable-product inventory control respectively. The local matching, startup and chip-shortage equations remain illustrative and unvalidated; matching-model equilibrium conditions, quantitative startup-model provenance, and an ECB forecasting-model mapping were not established.

Run `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_physical_sources_v14.py`. These checks verify receipt hashes, confirm the current non-metadata content matches the recorded prechange canonical SHA-256 hash (stable JSON with sorted keys and compact separators), without requiring Git history, and independently derive Greenshields capacity, weak-Allee growth sign and BZ first-step stiffness. They do not prove scientific fidelity or repair failing numerical defaults.

Other audit candidates containing broad repository URLs, mixed references or suspect DOI identities remain unresolved, not certified by omission. A source-backed numerical reconstruction needs explicit equation/table mapping, dimensions, boundary/initial conditions, parameter provenance, suitable numerical integration, conservation/positivity checks and comparison with published results. Existing model content is preserved pending that work.
