## Numerical evidence and work limits

`tests/test_library_candidates_third_science.py`: **35 passed**. Every retained stock at every default output is compared with independently handwritten numeric equations. DOP853 references ten systems; independently implemented BDF references Akzo while the engine uses Radau. Analytic solutions, published constants, invariants and controlled scenarios supplement agreement. All nine fixed-step examples pass coarser-grid halving checks. Both adaptive examples improve against the independent reference when tolerances tighten. These tests do not reuse the saved equation strings or coefficients as numerical reference definitions.

All eleven pass input inspection and report complete consistent symbolic units. Maximum full-grid state-amplitude-normalized discrepancy is approximately1.80e-8 (Chapman), subject to the exact evidence receipt. Default explicit ordinary-work estimates are40746–560014; adaptive Akzo and laser workloads are about118650 and192322. No engine caps were raised. Settings changed beyond the reviewed domains require new numerical checks.

Two coarse oscillatory grids initially missed the strict near-zero trajectory check; the retained bubble and charged-particle steps were halved, preserving equations. The Chapman horizon was extended to exhibit its derived stationary state. The Cahn-Hilliard scenario uses a specified asymmetric smooth perturbation so perfectly symmetric zero sites do not depend on amplified floating-point symmetry breaking. No hidden noise, positivity clamp, state reset or threshold adjustment was introduced to force a desired behavior.

Numerical conformance and dimensional consistency are not scientific calibration. Examples explicitly state when they omit physical effects, use normalized coefficients, eliminate a solvable constraint or discretize a continuum law. Akzo in particular reproduces the published benchmark equations; it does not repair their empirical/non-elementary reaction interpretation or add native general DAE support.

## Duplicate screening and mechanism distinctions

The tests dynamically scan active model JSON and every other staged candidate batch, including the second twenty and third engineering batch. They reject exact identity/title/equation duplicates and conservative expanded stock-derivative AST matches. Parameter expressions are expanded and stock names normalized. This is not proof of inequivalence under arbitrary algebraic transformations, state reordering or units changes, so related mechanisms were inspected manually too.

The active Allen-Cahn model is a nonconserved local order-parameter relaxation; Cahn-Hilliard transports conserved chemical potential with a fourth-order spatial mechanism. The active elastic-rebound model triggers stress release above a threshold; the new friction model has continuous contact-age healing and rate/state stability. Generic spring models lack gas pressure-volume work and its adiabatic invariant. Existing Ekman layers use Coriolis/mixing momentum; the Lorentz particle exposes charge-dependent gyromotion, positions and electric work. Existing urban ozone/health examples do not contain the four Chapman reactions. The new optical cavity retains a driven complex phase response; it is not the existing scalar Schloegl chemical equation. No source-complete bubble, polytrope, magnetic-moment, Kerr, Akzo or laser mechanism was found. Coordination reserved these scientific mechanisms away from the third engineering plant/controller batch.
