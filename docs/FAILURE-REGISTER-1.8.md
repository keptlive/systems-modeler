# Failure register — 1.8

Every one of the 86 public definitions that failed in 1.7 has an individual causal diagnosis, source boundary and current status. The full audit now completes 1,031 of 1,078 models; 47 fail explicitly. There are no newly failing defaults. Thirty-nine previous failures now complete, but completion alone does not establish source fidelity, calibration or physical correctness. Two additional inert models, Morris–Lecar and Oregonator, have restored and independently checked dynamics.

The [machine-readable register](../operations/failure-register-v18.json) binds all 86 cases to exact before/after hashes and the final full-library audit. The [first source partition](CORPUS-REPAIRS-1.8-B.md) and [second source partition](CORPUS-REPAIRS-1.8-C.md) retain equations, original-source evidence, refinement probes, independent references and remaining requirements. The [engine report](ENGINE-CAPABILITIES-1.8.md) explains root errors versus blocked values.
