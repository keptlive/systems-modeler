# Stock & Flow 1.7 — evidence and limitations

## Model investigation

Three Sol 6.1 reviews partition all 134 previously failing public models exactly once. The A/B/C ledgers record diagnosis, original-source evidence, exact before/after hashes and unresolved scientific questions. Sixty-four definitions changed, including mechanical symbol repairs, restored source equations/tables, and honest attribution/limitation corrections. The full 1,078-model default-horizon audit is 992 completed / 86 failed, compared with 944 / 134; no previously completing model regressed.

Completion is only numerical evidence. Morris–Lecar and Oregonator now fit the capacity budget but have no implemented derivatives and remain inert. Their receipts explicitly reject a scientific-success interpretation. Some other completed trajectories have physical-domain violations or source-authored coarse Euler drift. For example, restoring the original single-pendulum Euler grid preserves approximately 83.7% energy drift; independent RK4 invariant checks distinguish fidelity from accuracy. Unsupported or ambiguous equations remain failures rather than receiving guessed coefficients, initial populations or zero fallbacks.

See [partition A](CORPUS-RECONCILIATION-1.7-A.md), [partition B](CORPUS-RECONCILIATION-1.7-B.md), [partition C](CORPUS-RECONCILIATION-1.7-C.md), and [independent review](INDEPENDENT-REVIEW-1.7.md). Two production-only failures have ambiguous zero-denominator behavior after mechanical name correction; those saved models remain untouched.

## Engine and interface

The shared capacity contract accepts up to 100,000 intervals / 100,001 samples while retaining the 500-element and one-million-work-unit ceilings and adding a one-million-retained-scalar-value ceiling. Hidden stages, stochastic calls and RK4 cost still count. Solver equations are unchanged. API diagnostics, MCP descriptions and editor/analytics guards agree.

Analytics now leads with a selected-series result, recorded trace and eight readable metrics. All comparison, behavior, correlation and peak sections remain available in disclosures. Long numbers stay intact, tables scroll explicitly, final-value names wrap independently, and fullscreen/mobile layouts have deliberate spacing. Fullscreen event binding, close/focus behavior and chart selection synchronization are repaired. Export builds a styled clone with a complete SVG-native legend and offers PNG/SVG links in a preview dialog. See [design and browser evidence](ANALYSIS-STUDIO-1.7.md).

Numerical completion, source fidelity, calibration and scientific validity are separate claims. The unresolved corpus remains visible and documented.
