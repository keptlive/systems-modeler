The statewide sum and each original initial fraction recover every individual town exactly. The discrete Euler map has the same linear property. The 351 outputs are therefore preserved, rather than replaced by one representative town. Native `DELAY N(input,time,initial,order)` is reordered to the runtime's `DELAY_N(input,time,order,initial)`. Vensim's [DELAY N documentation](https://www.vensim.com/documentation/fn_delay_n.html) confirms the constant-duration exponential-chain semantics.

The original SEIR initial stocks specify cohort sizes but do not uniquely specify their ages. The repaired model declares a uniform-age scenario as a model input: incidence is 20/day on [-5,0) and 10/7/day on [-12,-5). Those windows integrate to exactly 100 exposed and 10 infectious. Distinct nested delay initial values preserve that history at each boundary. No missing historical infection series is claimed to have been recovered.
